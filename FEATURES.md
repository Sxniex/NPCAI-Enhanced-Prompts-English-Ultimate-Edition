# NPCAI Enhanced Prompts – English Ultimate Edition

A complete prompt-engineering overhaul for **NPCAI v3.1.1** designed to deliver significantly more immersive, intelligent, and realistic AI conversations for LSPDFR.

Unlike traditional modifications, this project does not modify NPCAI's source code, DLL files, or internal systems. Instead, it completely redesigns the behavior of the AI through advanced prompt engineering while maintaining full compatibility with the official NPCAI framework.

The English Ultimate Edition is the result of extensive real-world gameplay testing, continuous refinement, and hundreds of prompt improvements focused on realism, immersion, conversational consistency, procedural authenticity, and personality diversity.
